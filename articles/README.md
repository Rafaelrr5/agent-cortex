# Articles

Short pieces on working with AI coding agents. Each one is anchored to something
in this repo you can read and run, so the claims are checkable rather than
rhetorical.

| Article | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| [Implemented and tested is not evidence](implemented-and-tested-is-not-evidence.md) | Why an agent's closing summary cannot be trusted, and what to read instead |
| [Why your agent forgets everything](why-your-agent-forgets-everything.md) | Why every session starts from zero, and what a memory store has to get right |
| [Five agents, one repo](five-agents-one-repo.md) | Running concurrent agents on one codebase without them overwriting each other |
| [Why brand skills matter more than brand guidelines](why-brand-skills-matter.md) | The business argument for executable brand rules: review cost, not aesthetics |

Each follows the same shape, because it is the shape that survives contact with
a reader who is busy:

1. The concrete problem, as a situation from your own week.
2. The naive approach, and precisely where it breaks.
3. The mechanism, with the smallest real example.
4. One measurement, or an honest "not measured yet".
5. What it does **not** solve.

The fifth section is the one that matters. A piece with no limitations section is
marketing.
