# Article backlog

Short teaching pieces, 600-900 words each. Written from the top of this list,
which is ordered by how often the question actually comes up — not by what is
most interesting to write.

Every piece needs a **runnable anchor** in this repo: a file a reader can copy
and run. A piece whose example does not exist yet is not ready to write.

## Shape

1. The concrete problem, as a situation from the reader's own week.
2. The naive approach, and precisely where it breaks.
3. The mechanism, with the smallest real example.
4. One measurement, or an honest "not measured yet".
5. What it does **not** solve.

The audience includes people who have never opened a terminal. Define each term
once, in a clause, the first time it appears.

## Note on #4

The argument that lands with a non-technical manager is not aesthetic
consistency, it is **review cost**: the asset arrives already on-brand, so the
approval loop that used to take days stops existing. Lead with that. Colour
tokens go second, and briefly.

## Adding to the queue

Add a row when someone asks you the same question twice. That repeat is the
whole selection criterion — an invented curriculum produces pieces nobody was
waiting for.
